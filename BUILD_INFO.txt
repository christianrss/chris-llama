Chris Llama source package

Generated binaries and object files are not part of the source tree. Builds are
written under build/ and can be reproduced with:

  make BACKEND=cpu

or, after installing AdaptiveCpp:

  ./scripts/setup_adaptivecpp.sh
  make BACKEND=acpp ACPP_TARGETS=generic

AdaptiveCpp source used by the bootstrap:
  https://github.com/christianrss/AdaptiveCpp

Related AdaptiveCpp testbed:
  https://github.com/christianrss/tests-acpp
